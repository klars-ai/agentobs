# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Treating disagreement as a technical question rather than a personal one
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change without it being an insult
- Being specific: a reproduction beats an assertion, a measurement beats an opinion
- Saying when you were wrong, and moving on without ceremony

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Demeaning comments about someone's identity, background, or level of experience
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **contact@klars.ai**. Reports are read by the maintainers and
kept confidential.

Responses are proportionate: usually a private word, sometimes an edit or removal
of a comment, and for repeated or serious behaviour a block. Maintainers who do not
follow this document are subject to the same consequences as anyone else.
